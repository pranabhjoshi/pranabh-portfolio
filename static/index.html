About Me

Hi! I'm a junior at the University of Washington, double-majoring in Informatics and Political Science. As an aspiring Product Designer, I'm passionate about using design to drive positive social change by bridging technical and human perspectives to create user-centered solutions. Growing up in Spokane, I saw how issues like wildfire smoke hit lower-income neighborhoods harder than wealthier ones, not because of nature, but because of inequity in their homes or neighborhoods. Design affects us all, and with proper empathy, I'm passionate about designing more accessible, equitable systems that serve everyone.
